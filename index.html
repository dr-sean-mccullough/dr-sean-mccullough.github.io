Greetings

I am a technology architect and graduate from the Rhetoric and Composition PhD program at Texas Christian University. My academic journey is fueled by an interest in investigating the intersection of artificial intelligence, human computer interaction, and knowledge production through writing.

My primary research goals explore how computer infrastructures—and, recently, generative AI—mediate knowledge. I completed my PhD in Rhetoric and Composition at Texas Christian University, complemented by an extensive career in technology consulting.

About Me

A brief introduction to my background and journey.

I am a scholar and technology professional interested in the ways emerging technologies shape the means of persuasion: how we make knowledge.

Despite moving into a more corporate setting over the past several years, I have always had a foot in academic spaces: reading new articles, collaborating with scholars on chapters in edited collections, and finishing my PhD while working a 9-5.
