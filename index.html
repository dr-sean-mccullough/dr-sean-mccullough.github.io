Greetings

I am a technology architect and graduate from the Rhetoric and Composition PhD program at Texas Christian University. My academic journey is fueled by an interest in investigating the intersection of artificial intelligence, human computer interaction, and knowledge production through writing.

My primary research goals explore how computer infrastructures—and, recently, generative AI—mediate knowledge. I completed my PhD in Rhetoric and Composition at Texas Christian University, complemented by an extensive career in technology consulting.

1. Executive Overview

My research investigates how computer infrastructures—and specifically semantic generative AI layers—shape human meaning-making and professional writing production. By looking at software systems through a rhetorical lens, I analyze how interfaces, databases, and language models serve as active agents in framing knowledge.

2. Current Strategic Initiatives

Generative Writing Substructures

Analyzing how text generation pipelines alter standard composing workflows, user agency, and stylistic uniformity across enterprise and academic environments.

Infrastructural Rhetoric

Mapping hidden software architectures, cloud environments, and configuration dependencies as rhetorical sites that actively govern information flows.
